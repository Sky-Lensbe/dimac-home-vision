# Dimac

Build a premium Dutch-language marketing website for Dimac BV, a local renovation and construction company in Belgium. Goal: create a trustworthy, conversion-focused website/prototype that can later be handed to a developer for technical integration. The site should feel premium, local, reliable, practical, and craftsmanship-driven. Target audience: homeowners in the region of Diest, Scherpenheuvel, Tessenderlo, Halen and surroundings who are considering a renovation, bathroom renovation, kitchen renovation, roof works, technical installations, renewable energy, or a larger total renovation.

Core positioning:
Dimac is not just a contractor for separate jobs. Dimac keeps the overview from preparation to finishing, with one contact person, coordinated trades, technical preparation, and clean execution. The main message: "Eerst duidelijkheid. Dan beslissen." and "Geen losse werken. Wel één kloppend geheel."

Tone of voice:
Dutch/Flemish, clear, trustworthy, sober, premium, not salesy, not hype-driven. Use direct language for homeowners. Avoid generic marketing fluff. Emphasize clarity, coordination, preparation, technical correctness, finishing quality, and trust.

Visual direction:
Clean premium construction/renovation aesthetic. Use warm off-white backgrounds, charcoal/dark grey text, subtle beige/stone tones, refined accent color such as muted gold/copper/sand. Lots of whitespace, strong typography, sharp section hierarchy, premium cards, subtle lines, large imagery placeholders. Avoid cheap stock-photo feeling. Use image placeholders with labels like "Projectbeeld keuken", "Projectbeeld badkamer", "Detail afwerking", "Technieken in uitvoering", "Dakwerken" so real photos can be replaced later.

Structure:
Create a responsive multi-section website with a strong homepage and clear navigation. Navigation items: Home, Diensten, Werkwijze, Realisaties, Woningscan, Over Dimac, Contact.

Homepage sections:
1. Hero section
Headline: "Renoveren zonder losse eindjes."
Subheadline: "Dimac begeleidt renovaties van voorbereiding tot afwerking, met één aanspreekpunt en overzicht over elk detail."
Primary CTA: "Vraag een gesprek aan"
Secondary CTA: "Bekijk onze werkwijze"
Add small region line: "Actief in Diest, Scherpenheuvel, Tessenderlo, Halen en ruime omgeving."

2. Problem recognition section
Title: "Een renovatie loopt zelden vast op één werk."
Copy: Explain that kitchens, bathrooms, roofs, electricity, plumbing, heating, finishes and planning all influence each other. What is chosen in the showroom must be technically prepared correctly on site.

3. Trust proposition section
Title: "Eén partij die het overzicht bewaakt."
Create 3 cards:
- Voorbereiding: technische keuzes, timing, materialen and site preparation.
- Uitvoering: coordinated trades, clear communication, one contact person.
- Afwerking: details, transitions, alignment and clean completion.

4. Services overview section
Title: "Voor renovaties waar alles moet kloppen."
Create service cards for:
- Totaalrenovatie
- Badkamers
- Keukens
- Dakwerken
- Elektriciteit binnen renovaties
- Sanitair en technieken
- Hernieuwbare energie
- Afwerking en maatwerk
Each card needs 2-3 sentence explanation and a CTA "Meer over deze dienst".

5. "Wat je niet ziet" section
Title: "Wat je niet ziet, maakt vaak het grootste verschil."
Explain technical preparation, correct connections, waterproofing, ventilation, alignment, layers under the floor, and coordination before walls close.

6. Woningscan teaser section
Title: "Twijfel je waar je renovatie best begint?"
Explain a fixed-price home scan per square meter, deductible from the final project if the works are assigned to Dimac. Mention that the scan creates overview before decisions are made.
CTA: "Ontdek de woningscan"

7. Realisaties section
Show a grid of 4 project cards with placeholder images and captions:
- Badkamerrenovatie met zorg voor afwerking
- Keukenrenovatie waar technieken en maatwerk samenkomen
- Dakwerken met aandacht voor details
- Totaalrenovatie met één duidelijke planning

8. Review/trust section
Title: "Vertrouwen ontstaat door duidelijkheid."
Use 3 testimonial placeholder cards, with realistic but clearly placeholder Dutch quotes, such as "Dimac dacht mee van bij de voorbereiding en hield het overzicht tijdens de uitvoering." Do not present them as verified reviews; label them as voorbeeldreview/placeholder.

9. Contact CTA section
Title: "Wil je weten wat er technisch en praktisch nodig is voor jouw renovatie?"
Copy: "Vertel ons kort wat je wil aanpakken. Dan bekijken we samen welke stappen logisch zijn."
CTA: "Neem contact op"

Pages/sections detail:
- Diensten page: each service gets a dedicated block with homeowner pain points, what Dimac does, and why coordination matters.
- Werkwijze page: show a 5-step process: Kennismaking, Analyse of woningscan, Voorstel/offerte, Planning & voorbereiding, Uitvoering & oplevering.
- Woningscan page: explain the scan, when it is useful, what the client receives, and why it avoids wrong assumptions. Include FAQ.
- Realisaties page: project grid with filters/categories, placeholder content only.
- Over Dimac page: position as a local renovation partner with craftsmanship, technical overview, and clear communication. Mention team/people placeholders.
- Contact page: simple form with name, email, phone, postcode/region, type of project, short description, preferred timing. Add contact cards and region text.

UX/conversion requirements:
- CTA repeated after major sections.
- Sticky or clear top navigation on desktop and mobile.
- Mobile-first responsive layout.
- Forms can be frontend-only for the prototype, no backend needed.
- Make it easy to replace text and images later.
- Use semantic section names and clean component structure.
- Keep copy in Dutch.
- Avoid fake claims like "20 years experience" unless written as placeholder.
- Do not use real logos or copyrighted images unless placeholders are used.

Design details:
- Premium typography, strong spacing, elegant card layout.
- Use subtle animations only: fade-in/slide-up on sections, hover effects on cards/buttons.
- Buttons should look refined, not flashy.
- Include a visual "renovatieflow" component showing how separate works become one coherent whole.
- Add a footer with navigation, services, region, and CTA.

Deliver a polished first prototype that looks like a serious renovation company website, not a generic template.
